Important Academic Note on Similarity vs. Plagiarism

Text similarity calculated by TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity measures mathematical token and vector overlap. A high score does not automatically confirm intentional plagiarism: legitimate direct quotations, shared bibliography citations, mathematical formulas, and standard technical idioms naturally increase similarity scores. A qualified academic evaluator must review the context.
